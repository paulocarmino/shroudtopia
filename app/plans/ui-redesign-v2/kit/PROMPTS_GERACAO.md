# Prompts de geração — Shroudtopia V2

Ferramenta: geração nativa de imagens. Uma chamada por asset. O cenário é opaco; santuário e medalhão foram solicitados com fundo transparente. A paisagem fornecida como referência às duas últimas chamadas é `shroudtopia-mist-valley.png`, criada na primeira chamada. Os originais gerados foram copiados para este pacote sem recorte nem recoloração.

## shroudtopia-mist-valley.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: actual production background illustration for a compact desktop companion app to the fantasy survival building game Enshrouded. This is an ART ASSET ONLY, NOT a UI mockup, NOT a redesigned screen.
Create an original beautifully art-directed painterly fantasy environment: a lost green valley of ancient rough-hewn stone ruins submerged in luminous blue-green fog at twilight, with a small warm amber beacon glowing from a distant sheltered stone arch, lush dark fern silhouettes at the very bottom edges, stepped cliffs and an ancient watchtower silhouette in the distance. Mood: magical, quiet, handmade, inviting exploration and rebuilding, a sanctuary surviving the mist. Stylized painted game concept art with broad shapes, convincing volumetric atmosphere, exquisite restrained light, visible brushwork, NOT a photograph and NOT generic photoreal Unreal Engine ruins.
Canvas landscape about 1536x1024, 3:2. Composition for placement at the BOTTOM of an app's main content pane: the top 45 percent must be almost empty charcoal blue #101519, naturally fading down into fog; all recognisable architecture, cliffs and foliage in the lower 55 percent. The most beautiful readable ruined arch and the one warm beacon are in the lower right third, but leave a safe margin from all edges. Multiple foggy silhouettes across the lower half give depth. A low winding stone footpath at the bottom quietly leads toward the warm light. Restrained medium contrast in the illustration region so it remains visible at 500 pixels wide, do not make it so dark that it disappears. Upper region must stay almost flat dark for UI to sit over it.
Palette unified across a set of app assets: deep charcoal slate #101519, #1B2329, desaturated cyan fog #709FA9, weathered gray stone #6E7775, warm ivory highlights #E7DFCA, localized ember amber #DDA15A. One small warm light only. Avoid purple neon, flat brown sepia, gold everywhere, heavy black crush or saturated orange.
No text, no lettering, no logo, no watermark, no game UI, no characters, no swords, no floating interface elements, no borders. All artwork original, inspired by the game's mist/ruins/flame theme without reproducing an existing screenshot. Background is opaque, not transparent.
```

## shroudtopia-flame-shrine.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: a single isolated transparent painted game-art ornament for the empty middle region of a desktop app sidebar, NOT a UI screen or a mockup.
Input image: attached landscape is a STYLE AND PALETTE reference only. Do not edit that landscape, do not recreate it. Create one NEW isolated object matching its painterly stone, amber light and cyan mist mood.
Subject: an original small ancient sanctuary lantern: a low weathered gray stone pedestal bearing a small steady amber magical flame, sheltered by TWO simple upright angular stone ribs forming an incomplete protective arch. The silhouette subtly echoes an arch protecting a flame, visually compact and recognisable. Small fern or moss touches on the base, a few thin roots, gently chipped edges, restrained teal blue bounce light and warm ivory highlights. It should feel like a lovingly painted game prop from a lost fantasy kingdom, NOT a metallic crest and NOT a photoreal 3D product render.
View: near frontal with a little view of the top of the pedestal, no severe isometric distortion. Whole complete object, from flame tip to base, centered in a roughly square or slightly portrait canvas. It must occupy 80-88 percent of the image bounds, no enormous empty margins. Relatively broad silhouette that reads beautifully when displayed at 120-145 pixels wide; few large stones, no tiny busy runes or carvings.
Light: physically convincing beautiful warm amber flame against cool slate stone; medium bright so it actually remains visible on app sidebar #13191E at normal opacity. Flame is not a blazing bonfire, no sparks scattered across the image. Sculptural hand-painted concept-art brushwork, elegant simplified shapes. Body made of stone not shiny gold. Local light on the stone and a tiny soft halo immediately around the flame only.
Background: genuine transparent alpha, isolated cutout. No floor plane, landscape, sky, room, vignette, black rectangle or checkerboard. Preserve gaps through the arch as transparent too. No text, letters, logo, frame, official game logo, official exact in-game altar design, swords, people. Deliver just ONE object, not a sheet of variants.
```

## shroudtopia-world-medallion.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: ONE isolated transparent illustrated world medallion for a desktop game companion app; this is actual production artwork to be shown at 48 to 64 pixels, NOT a screen design.
Input reference landscape: STYLE AND PALETTE only. Match weathered stone, painterly fantasy game art, amber beacon light and desaturated blue fog. Create a NEW original icon, not a crop or recreation of the landscape.
Subject: a beautiful round weathered slate stone medallion with a very thin aged bronze rim and a few angular cuts. Within the circular face, a simple readable relief of a single ancient watchtower standing on one small mossy hill, a short path rising toward a glowing warm amber doorway. A hint of pale cyan fog lies behind the tower. The center tower silhouette is large and bold, not a panoramic miniature landscape. Bottom of the circle has ONE small amber inset stone. No sharp wings or giant spikes protruding from the rim. Restrained moss accents at lower edge, upper circle still clearly visible. Near front-facing view, almost flat heraldic disk with just enough carved sculptural depth; do not angle in perspective.
Style: painterly high-quality fantasy game inventory icon, large shapes and carefully grouped values so it reads at 48px. Stone dominates, metal only a fine trim. Highlights ivory, cool slate shadows, amber in one tiny light. Not a shiny gold coin, not a heraldic animal, not a photoreal material ball, not flat vector, not heavy photoreal 3D render. No intricate micro-details, no tiny writing or rune ring.
Composition: square canvas, one single centered object filling 88-92 percent of bounds, minimal transparent padding. Entire circular outline present and not clipped. No separate drop shadow behind it.
Background: genuine transparent alpha, all outside area clear. No scenery outside the medallion, no white or black background rectangle, no checkerboard drawn in. No text, no letters, no logo, no UI, no labels, no watermark, no variants. Original design, no official Enshrouded logo or official inventory icon reproduction.
```
